The Not Alone project touches on difficult university experiences that many students feel and share — moving to a new city, transitioning from high school, fears about grades and career paths, and more — with the goal of fostering “common humanity,” the sense that everyone struggles, encounters challenges, and fails. While the risk to you is very low, engaging with this project may bring up difficult emotions. If you're in crisis, we suggest not continuing — instead reach out to Student Wellness Services (403-210-9355) or the Distress Centre Calgary (403-266-4357). If this is an emergency, call 911.
